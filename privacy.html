Privacy Policy

Updated 21 November 2026

Calculator inputs

Our calculators work in your browser. TinyUseful does not require an account or send your entries to a server to calculate a result. We do not keep a history of your calculations.

Copying and sharing

Copy result puts the displayed result on your clipboard. In Tip and Bill Split, Copy link or Share creates a link containing the amounts you entered. Anyone who receives the link can read those amounts. They appear after the # in the address, which browsers omit from the request to our server. A shared link can remain in your browser history, clipboard or messages.

Display preferences

If you choose a light or dark theme, we save that choice in your browser’s local storage so it applies on your next visit. Clearing site data removes the saved preference.

Hosting and audience measurement

Cloudflare delivers the site, including its fonts, and helps protect it from abuse. It processes connection information such as your IP address and the requested page. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for page views and loading performance. This analytics service does not use cookies. Cloudflare privacy policy.

Google AdSense

Our pages request Google’s AdSense script when you open them, including when no ad appears. That request gives Google connection information such as your IP address and browser information. Depending on your region, consent choices and browser settings, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve ads, measure them and prevent abuse.

You can review Google’s data practices and manage ad personalisation. How Google uses data from partner sites · My Ad Center.

Search and AI crawlers

We publish our crawling preferences and use Cloudflare to manage access for automated visitors. Current crawler rules.

Privacy questions and requests

TinyUseful is operated in Prague, Czech Republic. If you have a privacy question or want to make a request about your data, email contact@tinyuseful.app. When you contact us, we receive the address and information you include so we can respond.

We update this page and its date when our practices change.
